New York's publication requirement

New York is one of the few states that requires new LLCs to publish a notice of formation. Within 120 days, you must run it in two newspapers chosen by the county clerk for six consecutive weeks, then file a Certificate of Publication ($50). Newspaper costs depend on the county and are much higher in Manhattan. If you skip it, the LLC's authority to do business in New York can be suspended.

New York registered agent

In New York the Secretary of State is the default agent for service of process, and it forwards papers to the address you list. Many companies also appoint a registered agent so documents reach a staffed address and are scanned the same day. We can fill that role and remind you of your biennial statement.
