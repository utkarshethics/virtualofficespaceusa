How to form a US LLC as a non-resident

You do not need to be a US citizen or resident to own a US LLC. Here is the full path — state, registered agent, filing, EIN without an SSN, US address and banking — and the ongoing filings to keep it in good standing.

1. Choose a state

Most non-resident founders choose Wyoming for its low ongoing cost and privacy, or Delaware if they plan to raise venture capital. If you will have staff, an office or inventory in a particular state, you may also need to register there. Compare the two in Wyoming vs. Delaware LLC.

2. Appoint a registered agent

Every LLC needs a registered agent with a physical address in the formation state. As a non-resident you cannot fill this role yourself.

3. File the Articles of Organization

File with the Secretary of State and pay the state fee — for example $100 in Wyoming, $110 in Delaware, $125 in Florida (including the registered agent designation) or $300 in Texas. Then sign an Operating Agreement that sets out ownership and how the LLC is run.

4. Get an EIN without an SSN

Apply to the IRS with Form SS-4. If the responsible party has no SSN or ITIN, the online application is not available; international applicants can apply by phone (+1 267-941-1099), by fax or by mail, writing "Foreign" on line 7b. Full steps are in How to get an EIN for an LLC.

5. Set up a US address and bank account

Banks and payment processors ask for a US business address. A virtual business address gives you a commercial street address and proof-of-address documents for your applications.

6. Stay compliant every year

  • File your state annual report or franchise tax report on time (see each state page for dates and fees)
  • Keep your registered agent active
  • For a single-member LLC owned by a foreign person: file IRS Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120 each year, even with no US income — the penalty for not filing starts at $25,000

We are not a law firm or tax adviser; talk to a US tax professional about your situation, especially if you have US-source income.
