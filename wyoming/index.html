Why founders choose Wyoming

Wyoming charges $100 to form an LLC and a $60 minimum annual fee, one of the lowest ongoing costs of any state. It has no state corporate or personal income tax, and the names of members do not have to appear in the public formation documents.

That combination makes it the default for e-commerce sellers, SaaS founders and consultants who live outside the US and want a simple holding or operating LLC.

Wyoming registered agent

Every Wyoming LLC must keep a registered agent with a physical Wyoming address. We act as your Wyoming registered agent, receive service of process and Secretary of State notices, scan them the same day and remind you before your annual report falls due on the first day of your anniversary month. If the annual fee is not paid within 60 days of the due date, the state can dissolve the LLC, so reminders matter.
