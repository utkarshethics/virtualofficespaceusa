Virtual mailbox for business with same-day scanning

Your business mail goes to a real US street address and is scanned the moment it arrives. Read it online, request a full scan, have it forwarded anywhere in the world, or shred it — without visiting a post office.

How mail scanning works

  1. Mail arrives at your US address and is logged against your suite number.
  2. We scan the outside of the envelope and notify you by email.
  3. You choose: open and scan the contents to PDF, forward the item, hold it, or securely shred it.

Packages from USPS, FedEx, UPS and DHL are received too, and can be forwarded internationally.

What a business mailbox handles well

  • IRS letters, including your EIN confirmation and tax notices
  • Secretary of State and franchise tax notices
  • Bank and payment-processor correspondence
  • Checks and invoices from customers
  • Marketplace and platform verification letters

Virtual mailbox vs. virtual office

A virtual mailbox is the mail-handling part of a virtual office. Our Digital Mail plan gives you the address and mailbox. The Business Pro plan adds registered agent service and bank-ready proof of address, which most new LLCs need.

USPS Form 1583

Federal rules require anyone receiving mail through a commercial mail receiving agency to file USPS Form 1583 and verify their identity. We send the form pre-filled and walk you through verification — see the Form 1583 guide.
