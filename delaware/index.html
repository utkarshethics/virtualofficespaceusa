Why founders choose Delaware

Investors and their lawyers know Delaware law, and the Court of Chancery has decades of business case law. If you plan to raise venture capital, issue stock options or sell the company one day, a Delaware C-corporation is usually expected.

For a small LLC without investors, Delaware costs more each year than Wyoming — compare them in Wyoming vs. Delaware LLC.

New: Delaware LLC tax rises to $400

Delaware raised the flat annual tax for LLCs and partnerships from $300 to $400, effective for the 2026 tax year. Because the tax is paid in arrears, the first $400 payment is due by June 1, 2027. Corporations are unaffected: their annual report and franchise tax remain due March 1.
