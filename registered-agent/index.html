Registered agent service for your LLC

Every US LLC and corporation must name a registered agent in its state of formation. We take that role, receive legal and state documents on your behalf, scan them the same day and keep your personal address off the public record.

What a registered agent does

A registered agent (called a resident agent or statutory agent in some states) is the person or company officially designated to receive service of process — lawsuits and subpoenas — and official notices from the Secretary of State, such as annual report reminders and franchise tax notices.

The registered agent's name and address appear on public state records, so anyone who needs to formally contact your company knows where to send papers.

For a full explanation, read What is a registered agent?

State requirements

  • A physical street address in the state where the company is formed or registered — P.O. Boxes are not allowed
  • Availability during normal business hours to accept documents in person
  • Consent to act as the company's agent

If you live outside that state — or outside the US — you cannot act as your own registered agent there, which is why most non-resident founders use a registered agent service.

Why use a registered agent service

  • Privacy — our address goes on the public record instead of your home address
  • Reliability — someone is always there to sign for documents during business hours
  • Speed — documents are scanned and sent to you the same day
  • Compliance — we remind you of annual report and franchise tax deadlines so you avoid late fees or administrative dissolution

Changing your registered agent

You can switch registered agents at any time by filing a change of registered agent form with the Secretary of State. Most states charge a small filing fee. We tell you which form your state uses and what to enter in the new agent fields.
