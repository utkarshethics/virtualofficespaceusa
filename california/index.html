Should you form in California?

California is one of the more expensive states to keep an LLC in: the $800 minimum franchise tax applies every year until the LLC is cancelled, whether or not it makes money. If you do business in California — staff, an office or significant sales there — you generally have to pay it anyway, even if you form elsewhere.

If you only want Silicon Valley credibility, you can form in another state and use a California business address. Get advice if you are unsure whether you are "doing business" in California.
