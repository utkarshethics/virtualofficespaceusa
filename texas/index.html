Why founders choose Texas

Texas has no personal income tax, a large and growing economy, and major tech hubs in Austin and Dallas. If you have customers, staff or inventory in Texas, forming there can be simpler than forming elsewhere and registering in Texas as a foreign LLC.

Texas franchise tax, explained

Texas LLCs are subject to franchise tax, but businesses with annualized total revenue of $2.65 million or less owe no tax for 2026 reports. That does not remove the filing: you still need to file by May 15 — including a Public Information Report — or face penalties and, eventually, forfeiture of the LLC's right to do business.
